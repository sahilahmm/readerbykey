# The BareBones design philosophy

Copyright (c) 2026 BareBones. All rights reserved.

1. **Bare.** Show only what is needed right now. No menus that hide the thing you came for, no jargon, no clutter.
2. **One hand.** Everything you do often sits in the thumb zone. The dynamic pill at the bottom morphs to match what you are doing: open, add, scan, share, archive, remove.
3. **One surface for feedback.** Messages, confirmations and undo live inside the pill, never in pop-ups over your content.
4. **Gestures first.** Swipe left anywhere to reach the Archive and right to come back; hold or drag where it saves a tap.
5. **Warm and calm.** Creamy Latte and Mocha for light mode, true black and cream for dark, one blue accent that always means "your action".
6. **Quiet craft.** Rounded concentric corners, smooth transitions, and haptics that feel like different materials.
7. **Private by design.** Works offline. Nothing is collected, uploaded or tracked.
