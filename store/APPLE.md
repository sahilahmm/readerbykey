# Apple App Store: answers for Reader by Key

**Needs:** Apple Developer Program membership (paid yearly; individual or organisation), a Mac or a cloud Mac to upload, and a signed build (the CI build is *unsigned*, see README).

**Name:** Reader by Key. **Subtitle** (30): Offline PDF, Word and text. **Bundle ID:** com.keybysah.reader. **Home screen name:** Reader.
**Category:** Productivity (or Books). **Price:** Free. **Age rating:** 4+ (no objectionable content, no web access).

**Privacy policy URL:** https://sahilahmm.github.io/readerbykey/privacy.html
**App Privacy ("nutrition label"):** choose **Data Not Collected**. The app also ships `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` declaring no tracking and no collected data.
**Export compliance:** `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false` is already set (the app uses no encryption of its own).
**App Review notes:** "Offline document reader. No login needed. Open the app, tap Open Document, and pick any PDF, Word or text file from Files."
**Review guideline notes:** works fully offline with real functionality (not a website wrapper: native file picking, sharing, Open In, local storage).
**EU Digital Services Act trader status:** App Store Connect also asks; declare non-trader only if this is not a business or profession for you.
**Screenshots:** 6.9" iPhone (1320x2868) at least 1; iPad needed because the app runs on iPad (or restrict to iPhone).
