**Full description** (4000 max):

Reader opens your PDF, Word (.docx) and text files and remembers where you stopped.

- One pill at the bottom, built for one-handed use. Open, share, rename, archive and remove files from the same pill.
- Word files keep their look: bold, italic, headings, lists, tables and pictures.
- Read any PDF as reflowing text that fits your screen, with adjustable size.
- Share a PDF as PDF or as Word.
- Password-protected PDFs, go to page, pinch zoom, night pages.
- Light or dark theme, your choice.
- Works fully offline. Reader asks for no permissions and has no internet access. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
- Shows up in Android's Open with list for PDF, Word and text files.
