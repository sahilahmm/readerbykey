# Privacy

The full policy is at https://sahilahmm.github.io/readerbykey/privacy.html (source: `docs/privacy.html`).

In short: Reader collects nothing. No accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and no internet permission. The only permission is the camera, used when you tap Scan; frames are processed on the phone and never leave it. Documents, recent list, reading position and theme stay in the app's private storage and are excluded from Android backups. A file leaves the phone only when you share it.
