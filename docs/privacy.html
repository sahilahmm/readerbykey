Reader by Key: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026 (app version 1.5.1)

Short version: Reader collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and it cannot connect to the internet.

Who is responsible

Reader by Key is made by Sah ("Key by Sah"), an individual developer. Contact: use a private report or an issue on the project page. The developer email shown on the Google Play listing also reaches me.

What stays on your phone

The documents you open (PDF, Word, text), their names, your reading position, zoom, your recent and archived lists, and your theme choice. All of it is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. I never receive it.

What is never collected

No personal data, no device identifiers, no advertising ID, no location, no contacts, no analytics, no crash reports sent anywhere. The Android app declares no permissions, including no internet permission (the scanner hands the photo over from your phone's own camera app and processes it on the device), and the iPhone app has no network code and no tracking.

Sharing

A file leaves your phone only when you tap Share and choose another app. That app's privacy policy then applies. Reader puts a temporary copy in its private cache for the share and clears it before the next one.

Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Because I hold no data about you, there is nothing for me to access, correct, export or erase. You control everything yourself: Remove or Archive a file inside the app, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it in Android settings to delete it all.

Children

Reader does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Third-party code

Reader includes the open-source libraries PDF.js (Apache-2.0) and Capacitor (MIT). Their licences ship inside the app and are listed in the repository. They run offline and send nothing.

Changes

If this ever changes, the date above changes and the release notes say so.
