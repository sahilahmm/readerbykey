### What's new in 1.7

- **Text recognition (OCR).** Scans are now read for text, in English and Bangla, fully offline.
- **Searchable PDFs.** The page picture stays exactly as scanned; the text sits invisibly underneath so you can search and copy it (including inside Reader).
- **Editable Word.** Scans saved as Word now contain real text you can edit, not just pictures.
- **Aa switch** in the pill (next to PDF and Word) turns text recognition on or off; off is faster and gives picture-only files like before.
- Reading takes a few seconds per page and shows "Reading page 2 of 5…" in the pill. Everything runs on your phone.
- The app is bigger because the recognition engine and language files are inside it.
