### What's new in 1.5.1

- **The scanner lives in the pill.** Tap Scan and your phone's own camera opens (better focus, HDR and flash than an in-app camera). Take the photo and Reader straightens and cleans it automatically.
- **Pages in the pill.** Each scan appears as a small thumbnail with its own remove button. Tap + for another page, ✓ when you're done, × to discard.
- **Processing, then PDF or Word.** The pill shows "Processing…", then PDF, Word and a discard button. The result opens straight away.
- **No camera permission.** Reader never holds the camera permission; the photo comes from your camera app and is processed on your phone.
- Word scans hold the page pictures (no text recognition yet).
