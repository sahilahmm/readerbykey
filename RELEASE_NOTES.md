### What's new in 1.3.6

- **Tools row above your files.** The theme button and Archive chip moved down from the top corner to sit right above the list, in thumb reach. It follows the list up and down as files come and go.
- **Smart swap.** The moment you select a file, the theme button turns into **Select all** (and **Clear** once everything is selected).
- **Drag to select.** Put your finger on a circle (or long-press a file) and slide over other files to select them in one move.
- **Pin up to 3 files** with the pin beside each circle. Pinned files sit at the very bottom, closest to your thumb.
- **Auto sort.** Recent shows the most-read files on top and unread or new files at the bottom, newest last. Pinned files follow the same pattern below the rest.
- **Recent holds 5 files.** When a sixth arrives, the most-read unpinned file moves to the Archive by itself, and the pill tells you with an Undo. (Older versions kept up to 10, so the extras move over once. Nothing is ever deleted.)
- **Archive grows with you.** It scrolls, sorts by when you archived, and groups by date (Today, This week, month names) or by type (PDF, Word, Text). Tap the Group chip to switch.
