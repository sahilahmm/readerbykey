## 1.9.1.2
- Word files now keep the structure: title, headings, sub-headings, paragraphs and bold text.
- Characters the reader can't place no longer leave "obj" boxes or gaps.
- The progress bar is now a fill that grows across the pill (no ring).
- You can keep adding photos one after another: the app finds the page edges in the background and never rebuilds the strip under your finger.
- If the phone closes the app while the camera is open, your pages come back.
